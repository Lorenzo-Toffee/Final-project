My final project for programming for digital arts

https://github.com/Lorenzo-Toffee/Final-project

## Description
A action based game where the player has to travel through various coridors like a maze to reach the end and defeat the 'boss' in order to win. Once the player does that there will be a little victory screen. The main inspiration comes from various Five Nights at Freddy's games mostly from 

## Features
- Feature 1
	- There will be 4 characters to choose from, the goal is to have each one have unique abilities that add fun to the play experience.
- Feature 2
	- There will be enemies to defeat, around 4 different enemies is the goal where each one has different move sets for the player to move around with.
- Feature 3
	- There will be little collectables to pick up that can restore health, boost damage for a little bit of time or speed.

## Challenges
